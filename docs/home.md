Doublespaced makes software for teachers.

The idea behind all of it is the same one: a teacher's work belongs to the teacher.
Nothing here asks for an account, keeps your data on someone else's computer, or stops working when the Wi-Fi does.

## Greatbook

A gradebook for teachers of any grade, from kindergarten through twelfth.

A grid of students by assignments that takes marks straight from the keyboard, standards and subjects as filters and colors, a report card for each student and grading period, and trends for one student or the whole class.
Benchmark tests, a behaviour record and a contact log are there for the classes that need them, and switched off for the classes that do not.

If you use Google Classroom, its grades download brings your class list, your assignments and your marks in together, and Common Core and the Next Generation Science Standards are built in, so a year can start without typing out a roster or a standard.

It runs as a desktop app on Windows and macOS and as an installable web app on a Chromebook, works offline, and keeps every score in a file you own.

Greatbook is free.
The one part that costs anything is keeping a copy of your gradebook in your own Google Drive, at <!--price--> once, and nothing else is held back.
